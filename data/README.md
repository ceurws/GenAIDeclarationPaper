# Data policy

- No CEUR-WS PDFs are stored here: CEUR-WS forbids third-party mirroring of
  its site or parts of it. Papers are referenced by their URL
  `https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-N/<file>.pdf`.
- Extracted declaration snippets are stored only for papers under CC BY 4.0
  (CEUR-WS volumes from summer 2019 on), with volume and paper URL as
  attribution. Older volumes permit use for private and academic purposes
  only; for them only the verdicts and labels are stored.
- Sample: seed, strata, stratum sizes and weights are stored with the sample.
- Third-party data enters only with a license or a written permission.
